Acquaintance Party

The acquaintance party held on August 2, 2025, at SU junior high school grounds was a nice experience, although problems arose from behind the scenes, but it was successful. I was part of the technical committee and was assigned to the karaoke booth. I was assigned to set up the equipment and was the person in charge of the booth. This was the first time I've been in charge of something that required me to interact with people, because most of the time it was only behind the scenes. The people I interact with are from some committee. This time it was different because of the gimmick for the acquaintance that requires students to go around to the different booths to have their tickets signed, so my partner and I were very busy. We faced problems, like where are the chargers for the speakers, the distance of the outlets, and also organising and controlling the crowd since it was near dinner and everyone was hungry, but we were able to manage. Overall, although the event was very rushed, it was a success. I don't know what happened to the original date for the acquaintance, but why did they move it earlier, giving us a little time to prepare, like 2 days max?
