Hibawanay

On August 26, I served as a marshal during the Hibawanay Quiz Bowl organized by the Department of Science and Technology (DOST) at KH 28. The quiz bowl brought together students from different schools to compete in answering challenging questions in science, mathematics, and other subjects. The objective of the activity was to encourage academic excellence and showcase the abilities of the students. My role as a marshal was to help maintain order, assist the organizers, and guide the participants throughout the event. Other marshals and staff worked alongside me to make sure everything ran smoothly and fairly.

While doing my task, I had the chance to read some of the test papers and questions, and I realized they were no joke. The topics were truly difficult and required serious preparation. I noticed how the participants studied hard and tried their best to answer, which impressed me and made me admire their dedication. At first, I thought the quiz bowl would just be another simple contest, but I was surprised by how intense and competitive it was, while still being respectful and motivating. What stood out to me was the determination of the students to push through even when the questions were really tough. The hardest part of my role was staying alert and attentive all throughout the event, but it was satisfying to know that I played a part in the success of the program.

This experience gave me a new perspective on academic competitions. I learned that success requires not only intelligence but also discipline, preparation, and courage. It reminded me to also take my studies seriously and put in extra effort when facing hard topics. In the future, I can apply this learning by managing my time better and approaching challenges with persistence. I also see the value of supporting events like this because they inspire students and strengthen their love for learning. If I could do this again, I would prepare more to be proactive in helping both the organizers and the participants.

Because of this activity, I am more inspired to respect the effort of students, to value the importance of preparation, and to be responsible in fulfilling my role in any activity.
